New Batch for Caregiving Course Starting Soon

July 15, 20262 min read

Admission is now open for our next Caregiving batch. The batch starts in January 2027, and seats are limited, so we encourage you to apply early.

This batch is for the Level 2 General Caregiving course, which follows the NSDA standard. It is the best place to start if you are new to caregiving.

What you will learn

  • Personal care and hygiene
  • Moving people safely, including wheelchair use
  • Health checks such as blood pressure and temperature
  • First aid and safety
  • Communication and teamwork

Who can apply

Anyone aged 18 to 35 who has passed SSC can apply. You do not need any caregiving experience. A kind and patient attitude matters most.

Caregiving is a skill that is needed in Bangladesh and in Japan. This course is the first step toward a stable career.

How to apply

Fill in the short form on our Caregiving application page. We will call you within 2 working days to answer your questions and confirm your seat.

If you have any questions, call us at 01712702072 or message us on WhatsApp.
