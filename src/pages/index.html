Who We Are

Community Skill Development Institute (CSDI) is a training institute in Mirpur, Dhaka. Since 2019, we have helped young people learn practical skills and move toward steady work. Our classes are small, so every student gets attention.

Learn More About Us

Why Choose CSDI

Here's what makes our training different.

Recognised Certificate

Our caregiving courses follow the NSDA standard, so your certificate is recognised in Bangladesh.

Learn by Doing

In every class, you practise real tasks with a trainer by your side, not only read about them.

Support After the Course

We help you take the next step, from finding work to applying for jobs in Japan with our partner.

Our Partner in Japan
