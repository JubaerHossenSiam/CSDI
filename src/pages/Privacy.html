Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information Community Skill Development Institute (CSDI) collects, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

1. Who we are

CSDI is a skill training institute in Mirpur-10, Dhaka. We teach caregiving, Japanese language, English and basic computer skills. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean CSDI.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information that we need. This includes:

  • Details you give us in the application form: your name, phone number, WhatsApp number, date of birth, gender, education, district and the course you choose.
  • Details you give us in the contact form: your name, phone number, email and message.
  • Student records, such as your student ID, photo, course, batch and certificate status, once you join a course.
  • Basic website data, such as the pages you visit and the type of device you use. We collect this with cookies.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Contact you about your application or question.
  • Manage your admission, classes and certificates.
  • Show the student verification page linked to your ID card.
  • Improve our website and courses.

We do not sell your information to anyone.

4. Student verification page

Each student ID card has a QR code. It opens a private page that shows your name, photo, student ID, course, batch and certificate status. This lets employers and partners check that you are a CSDI student.

This page uses a random link that cannot be guessed, and it is hidden from search engines. It does not show your phone number, address or date of birth. If you lose your ID card, tell us and we will turn off the old link and give you a new one.

5. Who we share information with

We only share your information when it is needed:

  • With NSDA or other official bodies, for assessment and certificates.
  • With our partner in Japan, Aqua Education and Consultancy Firm, only if you want placement support and agree to it.
  • With the law, if we are required to.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep application details for up to 2 years if you do not join a course. We keep student records for as long as needed for certificates and verification.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Some student records must be kept for certificates. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies

Our website uses a few cookies to work properly and to understand how visitors use it. You can turn off cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the site may not work as well.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact us

You can contact us by phone at 01712702072, on WhatsApp at 01917218424, by email at cibkafrul@gmail.com, or at our office: House 12, Road 3, Mirpur-10, Dhaka-1216, Bangladesh.
