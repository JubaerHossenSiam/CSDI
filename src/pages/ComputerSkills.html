Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the Basic Computer Skills course.

I have never used a computer. Can I join?

Yes. We start from the very basics, like using the mouse and keyboard.

Do I need my own computer?

No. You practise on our computers in the lab during class.

Will I learn Bangla typing?

Yes. You learn typing in both English and Bangla.
