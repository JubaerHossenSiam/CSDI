About CSDI

We help young people in Bangladesh build skills and find work.

Community Skill Development Institute (CSDI) teaches practical skills that prepare students for real jobs, from caregiving and Japanese to English and computer skills.

Our Story

CSDI was founded in 2019 because we saw a gap. Many young people wanted to work, but had no skills and no clear path to a job. Training was spread across many places, and it was hard to know where to start.

So we built CSDI to bring practical training, certification and career guidance into one place. Students get clear help at every step, from the first class to their first job.

Our Mission & Vision

What we do today, and what we are working toward.

Our Mission

To give young people practical skills that lead to real jobs, starting with caregiving.

Our Vision

To become a trusted skills institute in Bangladesh, known for the quality of our teaching and the success of our students.

Why We Start With Caregiving

Caregivers are needed in Bangladesh and in Japan. Our course helps you move from class to a job in three steps.

1

Learn a skill

Learn practical caregiving skills in class and in real practice.

2

Get certified

Earn an NSDA-approved certificate.

3

Start your career

Find work in Bangladesh, or in Japan through our partner.
